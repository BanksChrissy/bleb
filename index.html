USING THE ATLAS

A map, not a replacement for the source.

Individual references open issuing organizations or official journals. The separate PubMed discovery feed is automated and unreviewed—it is not a recommendation list. The year shown is the publication year, not a guarantee that the guideline remains the newest edition. An update note appears only when a publisher explicitly describes one.

Recommendation grades stay with the publisher.

Class of Recommendation, Level of Evidence, GRADE strength and certainty apply to individual recommendations—not an entire guideline.

Review the intended population.

Adult, pediatric, pregnancy and specialized care guidance cannot be used interchangeably.
